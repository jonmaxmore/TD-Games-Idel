# ADR 0008: PC only, 16:9

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-06
- Amends: [ADR 0007](0007-typescript-threejs-node.md) (drops the Capacitor mobile build) and the "web first, then mobile" plan in GDD §10

## Context
The owner decided to make the game for PC only, in 16:9.

## Decision
- **One screen shape.** The client lays everything out on a fixed 1280x720 stage and scales it to the largest 16:9 box that fits the window. Letterbox bars fill the rest.
- **The battle screen** has a top bar across the screen, the board on the left and a 360 px side panel on the right (cards, the selected disciple, secret arts, the wave button). The camera looks along the board, so the 18-tile path runs across the wide screen.
- **Mouse and keyboard.** 1-9 pick a disciple card, Space calls the next wave, U upgrades the selected disciple, P pauses, Esc clears the selection.
- **The build is the browser game.** A desktop wrapper (e.g. for Steam) comes later if needed. No Capacitor, no touch layout and no mobile store build.
- **Visual proof** (`npm run shots`) runs at 1280x720.

## Consequences
- **One layout to design, test and balance the UI for.** There are no safe-area insets and no portrait screens.
- **Design docs written for mobile need a pass before they drive any work.** Among them:
  - store IAP and the monthly card through app stores (`docs/design/economy.md`)
  - push notifications
  - "60fps on a mid-range phone"
  - the iOS launch in ADR 0004
- **Payments.** They must come from a PC channel (web payments or a desktop store) instead of the app stores. That is a product decision still open.
